## Output directory prefix

All prefixes share a common three-letter prefix **CCG** — **Combinational Circuit Generator**. The remainder is unique per generator.

Current generators and prefixes:

1. *FromRandomTruthTable* — CCGRTT  
2. *RandLevel* — CCGRCG  
3. *RandLevelExperimental* — CCGRCGE  
4. *NumOperation* — CCGRVC  
5. *Genetic* — CCGGA  
6. *Summator* — CCGTCSM  
7. *Comparison* — CCGTCC  
8. *Encoder* — CCGECR  
9. *Subtractor* — CCGTCSB  
10. *Parity* — CCGTCP  
11. *Multiplier* — CCGTCM  
12. *Demultiplexer* — CCGTCDMP  
13. *Multiplexer* — CCGTCMP  
14. *Decoder* — CCGDCR  
15. *ALU* — CCGALU
